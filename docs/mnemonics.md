# Curated anatomy mnemonics

Eight established cues apply to 86 of the current model targets. The registry is in `src/mnemonics.ts`; it matches reviewed structure names and collection, including side and source-name variants. Unmatched structures show no placeholder.

Study shows a compact disclosure with the cue visible. Opening it reveals the expansion, anatomical context, and source. Quiz and self-check mount the disclosure only after a submitted or revealed answer, inside feedback. The disclosure resets between targets. On short screens, opening it scrolls the explanation into its existing content area while the model and next action stay in place.

| Cue | Applies to | Verified scope |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Some Lovers Try Positions That They Can’t Handle | 16 carpal targets | Proximal row first, thumb to little finger in each row. Triquetrum appears as “triquetral” in the source model. [Texas Tech anatomy tables](https://anatomy.ttuhscep.edu/anatomytables/bones_alpha.html). |
| Tiger Cubs Need MILC | 14 tarsal targets | Talus, calcaneus, navicular, medial/intermediate/lateral cuneiforms, cuboid. The full list is not a single anatomical row. [Cuyamaca College guide, page 7](https://www.cuyamaca.edu/student-support/tutoring-center/files/student-resources/Krystal%20Identifying%20Structures%20of%20Skeletal%20System.pdf#page=7). |
| PEST OF | 8 cranial targets | Six bone types; eight bones because parietal and temporal are paired. [Kirtland Community College A&P study tips](https://libguides.kirtland.edu/c.php?g=1155275&p=10333523). |
| SITS | 8 rotator cuff targets | Supraspinatus, infraspinatus, teres minor, subscapularis; excludes teres major. [StatPearls: Rotator Cuff](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK441844/). |
| I Love Spaghetti | 18 erector-spinae targets | Iliocostalis, longissimus, spinalis, lateral to medial. Includes regional subdivisions, excludes semispinalis. [Pearson phrase](https://www.pearson.com/channels/anp/study-guides/central-nervous-system-thoracic-cage-vertebral-column); [UAMS anatomical order](https://medicine.uams.edu/neuroscience/education/medical-school-courses/human-structure-module/anatomy-tables/muscle-tables/muscles-of-the-back-region/). |
| DAB / PAD | 4 hand interossei targets | Dorsal abduct, palmar adduct, relative to the middle-finger axis. The hand explanation does not appear on foot interossei. [StatPearls: Hand Muscles](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK537229/). |
| Say Grace before Tea | 6 pes-anserinus muscle targets | Sartorius, gracilis, semitendinosus. Anterior-to-posterior order concerns their tendons at the medial proximal tibia. [Teaching phrase](https://pediatricteleradiology.com/tendons-pes-anserinus/); [cadaver and imaging study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5908566/). |
| LR6, SO4, the rest III | 12 eye-movement muscle targets | Lateral rectus: VI; superior oblique: IV; the other four eye-movement muscles: III. Excludes neck, abdominal, and eyelid muscles. [StatPearls: Visual Pathway](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK553189/). |

The phrases are established teaching cues; the explanatory wording is original. They supplement the existing anatomy notes and references. New entries need a reliable source, an exact expansion, explicit order/axis where relevant, and reviewed model-name matches. Do not generate a mnemonic just to populate an empty panel.

LOAF was considered but omitted from this initial set: grouped hand lumbrical targets combine muscles with different nerve supplies, and flexor pollicis brevis innervation varies. A future entry would need to state the usual pattern and its limits precisely rather than apply the cue to every thenar/hand target.

Validation covers current target matches, near-name exclusions, source-name aliases, compact rendering, and the absence of mnemonic markup in unanswered typed and multiple-choice quizzes.
