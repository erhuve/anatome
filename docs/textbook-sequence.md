# Textbook basis for quiz sections

Reviewed 5 November 2026. This is an original product recommendation, informed by published tables of contents. A textbook sequence provides a useful default; it does not establish a universal syllabus or demonstrate that one quiz order produces better learning.

## Sources checked

- [OpenStax, Anatomy and Physiology 2e, preface](https://openstax.org/books/anatomy-and-physiology-2e/pages/preface): six units; explicitly intended for a typical two-semester course. Use this as the default chapter reference because readers can access the linked chapters freely.
- [Pearson, Marieb Human Anatomy & Physiology, 12th edition, chapter listing](https://www.pearson.com/channels/anp/textbook-solutions/hoehn-12th-edition-9780138242732): current US edition credited to Hoehn, Haynes and Abbott. The [Marieb/Hoehn 11th-edition publisher contents](https://www.pearson.com/en-gb/subject-catalog/p/Marieb-Human-Anatomy-Physiology-Global-Edition-11th-Edition/P200000008877?view=educator) also confirms the broad organization into foundations, support/movement, control, maintenance and continuity.
- [WileyPLUS, Tortora & Derrickson, Principles of Anatomy and Physiology, 16th edition, contents](https://www.wileyplus.com/anatomy-and-physiology/tortora-principles-anatomy-physiology-16e-eprof20640/): independently confirms the broad sequence and separates axial from appendicular skeleton.

These sources agree closely enough to support a shared course order. They differ in chapter boundaries: Marieb combines the skeleton in one chapter and separates lymphatics from immunity; OpenStax integrates the senses into its somatic nervous-system material; Tortora gives senses their own chapter. Chapter numbers should therefore identify the book, rather than imply an interchangeable numbering system.

## Applying this to the current app

The following decisions are design inferences for this app, rather than claims made by the textbooks.

1. Make **unit → section → quiz** the primary navigation. Keep regions and individual structures as useful ways to customize a section. A large undifferentiated physiology category combines unrelated stages of the course.
2. Use six broad units: foundations; support and movement; nervous/endocrine control; transport and defense; respiration/nutrition/excretion; reproduction/development. Put tissue study early, then revisit relevant tissues in later systems.
3. Split skeletal practice into axial and appendicular sections. Within axial practice, progress through skull, vertebral column and thorax. Within appendicular practice, progress through shoulder girdle, upper limb, pelvic girdle and lower limb. The chapter introductions support these regional groupings: [axial](https://openstax.org/books/anatomy-and-physiology-2e/pages/7-introduction), [appendicular](https://openstax.org/books/anatomy-and-physiology-2e/pages/8-introduction).
4. Split muscle contraction from regional muscle identification. Regional practice can run head/neck, trunk, upper limb and lower limb, with finer regional sections where the model pool is large. OpenStax explicitly presents regional muscles from head to foot in its [muscular-system introduction](https://openstax.org/books/anatomy-and-physiology-2e/pages/11-introduction).
5. Assign questions explicitly to sections by what they assess. For example, sweating/feedback belongs to homeostasis; ATP/calcium/cross-bridges belongs to muscle contraction; neuronal depolarization belongs to neural signaling. A source URL or the old broad category alone is insufficient to establish section membership.
6. Keep each first pass within its chosen section. Offer mixed review after learners have selected the sections to combine; the textbooks' organization does not imply that random cross-course mixing is a suitable starting experience.
7. Show what a section actually contains. The app currently has five microscopy images, broad skeletal/muscle model collections, selected femur/humerus/scapula landmark locators, and a small factual question bank. A short available quiz must not imply that its whole textbook chapter is assessed. Sections without questions remain reference topics and do not increase quiz completion totals.
8. Preserve stable question IDs and saved results when changing navigation. A completed question remains completed; reorganizing material should not reset learning history.

The immediate improvement is organizing existing assessment honestly. Filling gaps in chemistry, organ systems, actions/attachments, joints, and full physiology is a separate content task.
