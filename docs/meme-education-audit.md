# Meme educational-value audit

Reviewed every app image visually against two requirements: humor and a specific, correct recall cue in the image itself. Study notes can explain and extend that cue, but cannot create missing educational value. This is an editorial assessment, not a measured learning-outcome claim.

**20 retained · 34 retired · 60 active quiz questions · 12 muscle/bone lessons.**

Retired images and lesson records remain in source for provenance. Existing quiz attempts still load; retired records are absent from browsing, search, new quizzes and direct meme routes. Their image fingerprints remain in duplicate detection.
