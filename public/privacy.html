Anatomeme privacy policy

Effective November 5, 2026. Applies to https://anato.meme.

Guest practice and browser storage

You can study without an account. Guest answers, review schedules, coverage, and preferences are saved in your browser. Signing in does not automatically upload guest history; you can explicitly import it into your account. Clearing this site's browser data removes local history, cached progress, preferences, and the saved sign-in session. It does not delete cloud account data.

Google sign-in and account progress

If you choose Google sign-in, Google provides account information such as an identifier, email address, name, and profile image to Supabase Auth, our authentication provider. Anatomeme uses your account identity to sign you in and associate study history with your account. The requested Google access is limited to basic sign-in and profile information; Anatomeme does not request access to Gmail, Drive, or your contacts.

Signed-in practice stores question identifiers, whether answers were correct, timestamps, collection passes, and history-import events in Supabase. Imports can include existing notes, checklists, and review schedules. This supports progress summaries, reviews, and continuity across devices. Account progress is also cached in your browser so pending changes can sync after reconnection. Custom selection and answer-mode preferences are saved on the device for each account.

Optional global leaderboard

You can choose to join the global leaderboard with a public nickname. If you join, anyone can see your chosen nickname, score, and rank. The board does not publish your Google name, email, account identifier, individual answers, or question history. Scores count distinct eligible 3D quiz targets answered correctly while signed in, including eligible answers before joining. Signing in alone does not join the board.

You can change your nickname or leave from the leaderboard page. Leaving removes your public listing; your nickname reservation and earned score remain private so you can rejoin. Account deletion also removes your leaderboard profile and score. Public information may already have been viewed or copied by others while you were listed.

Hosting and service providers

Cloudflare hosts the website and its anatomy assets. Supabase provides authentication and the progress database. Google handles Google sign-in. PostHog provides product analytics. Requests to these services can include technical information such as IP address, browser information, and request details needed to deliver, secure, and operate the service. Each provider also processes data under its own privacy policy.

Anatomeme does not include advertising and does not sell study history. Account history is not a public profile; database access rules restrict ordinary signed-in users to their own records. Authorized service administration can access stored data to operate the app and handle support requests.

Product analytics

Anatomeme uses PostHog to understand visits and improve study features. Analytics records pageviews, study modes and collections, viewed structure identifiers, quiz starts and completions, question identifiers, whether an answer was correct or revealed, answer mode, and selection counts. PostHog also receives technical information such as browser, device, referring site, and IP address. Its SDK stores an analytics identifier in browser storage to recognize return visits. When you sign in, analytics is linked to your Supabase account ID; signing out resets that browser identity.

Typed answer text, study notes, email addresses, names, and Google OAuth codes are not sent by this integration. Analytics URLs retain only recognized app navigation parameters. Automatic element capture, session recording, and automatic exception capture are disabled. Browser controls or extensions can block analytics; practice and account progress still work. PostHog processes analytics under its privacy policy.

Retention and your choices

Cloud history is retained so your account can resume practice. The current app does not have a self-service account deletion screen. To request access to or deletion of account data, contact raymondhuangluo@gmail.com using the email associated with your account. Identity verification may be needed before a request is acted on. Revoking Google's connection or signing out does not itself delete previously saved Anatomeme history. You can remove local data through your browser's site-data settings.

Do not put medical records or other sensitive personal information in study notes. Anatomeme is an anatomy study tool.

Changes and contact

This page will be updated when the app's data practices change. Questions about this policy or your data can be sent to raymondhuangluo@gmail.com.
