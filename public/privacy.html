Anatome privacy policy

Effective November 2, 2026. Applies to https://anato.meme.

Guest practice and browser storage

You can study without an account. Guest answers, review schedules, coverage, and preferences are saved in your browser. Signing in does not automatically upload guest history; you can explicitly import it into your account. Clearing this site's browser data removes local history, cached progress, preferences, and the saved sign-in session. It does not delete cloud account data.

Google sign-in and account progress

If you choose Google sign-in, Google provides account information such as an identifier, email address, name, and profile image to Supabase Auth, our authentication provider. Anatome uses your account identity to sign you in and associate study history with your account. The requested Google access is limited to basic sign-in and profile information; Anatome does not request access to Gmail, Drive, or your contacts.

Signed-in practice stores question identifiers, whether answers were correct, timestamps, collection passes, and history-import events in Supabase. Imports can include existing notes, checklists, and review schedules. This supports progress summaries, reviews, and continuity across devices. Account progress is also cached in your browser so pending changes can sync after reconnection. Custom selection and answer-mode preferences are saved on the device for each account.

Hosting and service providers

Cloudflare hosts the website and its anatomy assets. Supabase provides authentication and the progress database. Google handles Google sign-in. Requests to these services can include technical information such as IP address, browser information, and request details needed to deliver, secure, and operate the service. Each provider also processes data under its own privacy policy.

Anatome does not currently include advertising or a third-party behavioral analytics SDK, and does not sell study history. Account history is not a public profile; database access rules restrict ordinary signed-in users to their own records. Authorized service administration can access stored data to operate the app and handle support requests.

Retention and your choices

Cloud history is retained so your account can resume practice. The current app does not have a self-service account deletion screen. To request access to or deletion of account data, contact raymondhuangluo@gmail.com using the email associated with your account. Identity verification may be needed before a request is acted on. Revoking Google's connection or signing out does not itself delete previously saved Anatome history. You can remove local data through your browser's site-data settings.

Do not put medical records or other sensitive personal information in study notes. Anatome is an anatomy study tool.

Changes and contact

This page will be updated when the app's data practices change. Questions about this policy or your data can be sent to raymondhuangluo@gmail.com.
