Educational use

Anatomeme provides anatomy models, study material, and practice questions for learning. It is not medical advice, a diagnostic service, or a substitute for instruction from a qualified educator or clinician. Model coverage is incomplete, anatomical variation exists, and labels or study annotations can contain errors. Check important facts against your course materials and authoritative references. Progress and accuracy scores describe recorded practice; they do not certify mastery or readiness for an examination or clinical work.

Accounts and responsible use

You can practice as a guest or use Google sign-in to save account progress. Protect access to your Google account. Use the app lawfully, and do not attempt to access another person's records, bypass access controls, or disrupt the service. Avoid entering sensitive personal information in study notes.

Availability and saved history

Features and study content may change, and interruptions can occur. Anatomeme does not guarantee uninterrupted access, error-free content, or permanent storage. Browser data can be lost when site data is cleared, a device is reset, or browser storage is unavailable. Signed-in progress requires a working connection to sync. Keep your own copies of any study notes you need to retain.
